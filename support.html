Help & Support

My Worlds · your shows, movies and books, beautifully kept

Need a hand?

Email keepsakeapps@gmail.com and I'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days. If something looks wrong, a screenshot helps a lot.

Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. You'll get a link to choose a new one. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive in a few minutes.

What does the free version include?

Up to 3 shows or movies, 3 books and 10 photos, with every feature. Unlock everything is a one-time purchase that raises the limits to 200 shows or movies, 200 books and 250 photos. No subscription and no ads.

I bought the unlock but it's not showing

Open the gear menu → Unlock everything → Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Adding widgets

Press and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap the button at the top left, then Add Widget and search for Worlds. Choose Reading, Up Next, Mood Board, My Doors or Favorite Door. To change which world or book a widget shows, press and hold it and tap Edit Widget. Widgets update when you open the app.

Using My Worlds without signal

Everything you've already opened works offline, and changes save on your phone and sync when you're back online. Photos you add offline show a small cloud until they upload.

Sharing a world or reading with a friend

Open the world or book → gear → Share (or Buddy read), enter their email and pick what to share. They need a My Worlds account with that email. Anything you don't share stays private to each of you.

Backing up and restoring

Gear menu → Backup & restore. Download my data saves one file (with photos if you choose) plus a copy you can read anywhere. Restore from backup puts back anything that's missing, without changing or doubling what you have.

Deleting my account

Gear menu → Delete my account. This permanently erases your account, everything you've added and your photos. Download a backup first if you'd like a copy.
