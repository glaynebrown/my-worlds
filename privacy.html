Privacy Policy

My Worlds · effective October 7, 2026

The short version

  • My Worlds keeps your shows, movies, books, notes and photos so you can see them on your devices. That's all your information is used for.
  • No ads. No tracking. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.
  • You can download everything you've saved, or delete your account and all of it, at any time from inside the app.

My Worlds is made by Gabriella at Keepsake Apps, an independent developer. Questions? Email keepsakeapps@gmail.com.

What My Worlds saves

  • Your account: your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by Google's Firebase Authentication; I never see them.
  • What you add: your worlds and books, notes, quotes, favorites, reviews, ratings, reading dates, watch progress, wishlist, the name you choose to show, and your settings.
  • Your photos: pictures you add to boards, doors, backgrounds, covers and maps. They're made smaller on your phone before they're saved.
  • Sharing: if you share a world or start a buddy read, the email address you invite and the name you use in that share.
  • Your purchase: whether you've unlocked the full app. Purchases are made through Apple; I never receive your payment details.

What stays on your phone

  • Reading text from a photo: when you pull a quote or note from a picture of a page, the text is read on your phone and the picture is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Widgets: your widgets get a small copy of your doors, current book, next episode and a few board photos, kept on your phone only.
  • Backups: a backup you download is saved wherever you choose (your phone or iCloud Drive). It never goes to me.
  • Photos added without signal wait on your phone until they can upload.

Where it's kept, and who helps

Your account and everything you save are stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) on servers in the United States, and sent over encrypted connections. Security rules let only you read and change your own information. Things you share are visible to the people in that share.

A few services see small pieces of information only when you use certain features:

  • Book search sends what you type to Open Library (and Google Books as a backup) to find the book and its cover.
  • Fonts for the app's looks load from Google Fonts, and the tool that reads text from photos loads from jsDelivr. Like any website, these see your device's internet address when they're loaded.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple, which tells the app whether you’ve unlocked. Apple doesn’t receive your notes, photos or other content.

None of these are used for advertising, and My Worlds has no analytics or tracking tools.

Your choices

  • See or keep a copy: gear menu → Backup & restore → Download my data.
  • Change things: edit or delete anything you've added, any time.
  • Leave a share: stop sharing or leave a shared world or buddy read from its settings. You keep your own copy.
  • Delete everything: gear menu → Delete my account. This permanently erases your account, everything you've added and your photos.

If you'd like help with any of this, email keepsakeapps@gmail.com.

Children

My Worlds isn't meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly keep information from them. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email me and I'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Big changes will also be mentioned in the app.
